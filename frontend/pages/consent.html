Privacy and Consent Notice

Data Privacy and Protection

Before continuing, please review and understand how the information provided during this service will be handled.

Protecting user information and maintaining responsible data processing practices are important principles of this service. Information provided will be processed for password security assessment purposes.

This service is developed by the 4CSA11 researchers of the University of Cabuyao.

The identity of the Personal Information Controller responsible for this service must be confirmed with the appropriate university representative before publication of this notice.

For questions or privacy concerns, contact the designated privacy representative at rosebelle.egagamao@gmail.com .

Policy Version and Date: Version 1.0, effective 10/09/26.

Password Information Handling

The service is designed not to store or retain the actual password entered by the user.

Password inputs are temporarily processed to identify security-related characteristics, including password length, character composition, dictionary-word presence, and structural patterns.

Extracted password characteristics are used during the assessment process to generate security results and recommendations. The system is designed not to retain the original password as stored assessment data.

Under the proportionality principle in Section 11 of Republic Act No. 10173, personal data should be processed only when relevant and necessary for the stated purpose and retained only for as long as necessary, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Confidentiality and Information Security

All information processed by the service must be handled responsibly and protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or other inappropriate use.

Users should only provide information that they are authorized to evaluate and should avoid submitting passwords or information belonging to another person without proper permission.

Pursuant to Section 20 of Republic Act No. 10173, appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures must be implemented to protect personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, alteration, disclosure, and other unlawful processing.

User-created content must not be shared with commercial advertisers or other data services or companies for their own use or research, except where disclosure is legally required or otherwise lawfully authorized.

User Consent and Voluntary Participation

Providing consent is voluntary. Users may choose not to continue using this service if they do not agree to the terms stated in this notice.

By providing consent, users acknowledge that password-related characteristics may be analyzed to generate security assessment results and recommendations.

Processing activities must be based on a valid legal basis under Republic Act No. 10173. Where consent is relied upon, it must be freely given, specific, informed, and appropriately documented.

The applicable legal basis and consent procedure should be confirmed with the responsible Personal Information Controller and legal counsel.

Security Assessment Disclaimer

The generated assessment provides security insights based on detected password characteristics, patterns, and common vulnerability behaviors.

The results do not guarantee that a password is completely secure or impossible to compromise.

Recommendations are intended to help users understand potential password weaknesses and improve their password security practices.

Rights of the Data Subject

Under Section 16 of Republic Act No. 10173, data subjects have rights concerning the processing of their personal information, subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations.

These rights include the following:

a. Right to Information: The right to be informed whether personal information will be, is being, or has been processed, including relevant information about the purposes and methods of processing.

b. Right to Object: The right to object to certain processing activities under the conditions provided by law.

c. Right to Access and Rectification: The right to reasonable access to personal information and to request correction of inaccurate or erroneous information, subject to applicable legal provisions.

d. Right to Blocking or Erasure: The right to request blocking, removal, or destruction of personal information in circumstances provided by law.

e. Right to File a Complaint: The right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when an individual believes their personal information has been improperly handled or their data privacy rights have been violated.

f. Right to Damages: The right to seek indemnification for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal information, as provided by law.

For information about filing a complaint, visit the National Privacy Commission website or contact complaints@privacy.gov.ph .
