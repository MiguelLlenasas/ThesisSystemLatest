Privacy and Consent Notice

Data Privacy and Protection

Before continuing, please review and understand how the information provided during this service will be handled.

Protecting user information and maintaining responsible data processing practices are important principles of this service. Any information provided will only be processed for security assessment purposes.

Password Information Handling

The service does not store, save, or retain the actual password entered by the user.

Password inputs are temporarily processed only to identify security-related characteristics, including password length, character composition, and structural patterns.

Only extracted password characteristics are used during the assessment process. The original password is not included in stored information, shared records, or external databases.

Confidentiality and Information Security

All processed information must be handled responsibly and treated as confidential.

Users should only provide information that they are authorized to evaluate and should avoid submitting passwords or information belonging to another person without proper permission.

User Consent and Voluntary Participation

Providing consent is voluntary. Users may choose not to continue using this service if they do not agree to the terms stated in this notice.

By providing consent, users acknowledge that password-related characteristics may be analyzed to generate security assessment results.

Security Assessment Disclaimer

The generated assessment provides security insights based on detected password characteristics, patterns, and common vulnerability behaviors.

The results do not guarantee that a password is completely secure or impossible to compromise.

Responsible Use and Legal Considerations

Users are responsible for ensuring that submitted information does not violate the privacy, security, or rights of other individuals.

Unauthorized access attempts, password theft, or misuse of this service are strictly prohibited.

Service Processing Information

The service analyzes password-related characteristics to provide security insights, assessment results, and recommended improvements.

Consent Declined

You have chosen not to provide consent. You may exit this service or return if you decide to continue with the password security assessment.
