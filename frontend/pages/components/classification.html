VULNERABILITY
Password Vulnerabilities
Brute Force
Tries many possible password combinations until the correct one is found.
Example: Trying combinations like "abc123", "abc124", "abc125", and so on.
Dictionary-Based
Tries common words, passwords, or phrases from a prepared list.
Example: A password like "basketball" may be tried because it is a common word.
Rule-Based
Tries common changes or patterns made to familiar words or passwords.
Example: Changing "basketball" to "Basketball123!".

RISK LEVEL
Risk Level
Moderate Risk
The password has some weaknesses that may make it vulnerable to common attacks.
Example: A password is fairly varied but still contains a predictable pattern.
High Risk
The password has significant weaknesses that make it more susceptible to password-cracking attempts.
Example: A password uses a common word with a simple number or symbol added.
Critical Risk
The password has severe weaknesses and may be quickly compromised using common techniques.
Example: A very simple password such as "123456" or "password".
Reminder: The system does not try to crack or reveal your password. It analyzes the password's characteristics and patterns to determine its dominant vulnerability category and provide an understandable explanation of the result.
